It is 2536.

Technological progress is at an all-time high, as the discovery and confirmation of the Noösphere 88 years ago catapult the galactic scientific-epistemic community into overdrive, despite any threat of eldritch creatures emerging out of the Noösphere. 

Their findings and experiments end up in the hands of belligerents for the Corporate Crossfire; a known-galaxy-spanning cold war between the NanoTrasen Megacorporation, CYBERSun Inc., and thousands of others who are in for a pound when it turns hot. 

On the frontier, criminal networks like the Spinward Syndicate and its rivals deal in shadow war, while the coreworlds of the neoliberal Trans-Solar Federation and the communist Cygnian Accord ready for being dragged into the Crossfire.

There is only one way the cold war can end, nothing beyond an act of eldritch malice can change its course. It only remains to be seen what will spark it alight, and what will remain in its ashes.

But… That’s all a bit far away from the Maximón sector, a now-neutral backwater zone.

After its war for control between a triad of empires gone into a stalemate due to all three going under from the war, reparations have been long and drawn-out. All that remains are wreckage belts orbiting stars and planets, drifters and smugglers, people on the run, freighters taking a pit stop, tiny outposts of unaligned stations... and an alliance called Terrace & Terrance & Allied.

Under management of Terrace & Terrance Private Shipping, a C-list logistics and administration corporation managed by the eponymous siblings, T&T&A is a pseudo-corporate alliance of various organizations from across the spectrum. 

Noted for greeting everyone with a contract and a “Well, how much are you willing to work for hourly?”, T&T&A have carved out their own little slice of the world, breathing some amount of life within Maximón. Sure, such life is tenuous as their own disagreements threaten stability and larger entities notice, but the type they attract are just glad to not work under NTMC or CYBERSun. 

Few here will end up in the history records, if ever. Most are here to live a life, be it a good one or a long one. While Maximón is not truly outside of the Crossfire’s grasp, and that freedom from corporate rule comes in exchange with compromise, its the closest to living a free life without becoming a recluse. 

And one day, some day soon, *something beautiful will happen here.*
