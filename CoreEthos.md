Maximón is a fork from Macrocosm built from the ground up for roleplay, with interdepartmental drama being one of it's main draws. It's a setting that conveys a similar atmosphere to post-Soviet bloc art mixed with retrofuturistic technology and occult magic. The sector itself isn't part of the major corporate war other forks focus on, instead choosing a smaller scope to focus on the players and how they react to others.

For this sector; roleplay is– at its core– to play a role, to have buy-in to engage with the world as if its real and your characters like they're real, and to tell a collaborative story with others. This can take many forms, but Maximón is primarily focused on each player being a believable character that makes sense with its world, as to tell a story about people in a backwater sector making something meaningful out of what they do with other people. ERP is enabled for this fork, but is not the focus of playing the game, a player who doesn’t want to engage in ERP is perfectly welcome.

Mechanically, this fork makes significant changes to the core of SS14 mechanics as to make the inherent ludonarrative creation that SS14 is good at to focus on interpersonal interaction and drama. Making fun stories and a world to care about, without needing people to shoot at each other for it to be interesting or worthwhile. Departmentally, the station is restructured to accommodate the possibility of interdepartmental conflict, and to conform to new lore.

Speaking of which: lore-wise, the year is 2536 (current date + 510 years). The bigwigs in the corporate capitalist known-galaxy are embroiled in a long-standing cold war that promises to turn hot, as nation alliances scramble for relevancy and power, and occultist seek knowledge. Primarily pushed on by the absolutist NanoTrasen Megacorporation and the old Sol zaibatsu CYBERSun Inc, there are an infinite amount of smaller entities within this. You don't work for anyone major, or particularly near them. 

Welcome to the Maximón sector, a rebuilding neutral zone after its previous owners stalemated and left it in a state of destruction. Who you *do* work for is a coalition of minor entities, fueled by mutual compromise and nominal comradery. 

*Let's build something out of nothing.*
