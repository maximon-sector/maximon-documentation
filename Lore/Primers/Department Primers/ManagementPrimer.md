## Your Managing Group:
Name: Terrance & Terrace Private Shipping Inc. - *Frontier logistics, simplified!*

Org. Type: Limited Company

Leadership: Sibling *Co-CEOs* Terrance and Terrace Kohut.

HQ: Capital-class cruiser CC Man'O'War, in varying sites within the Maximón sector.

Specialty: Trading and shipping. Grey-market selling. Bureaucratic outsourcing. Wheelin' n' dealin'.

Summery:  
After a distant grandfather leaked some corporate assets down to two Carnivikin siblings with his overdue closed-casket funeral, the low-level bureaucrat and small-time PMC officer took their new micro-corp down to the now-peaceful Maximón sector. The epitome of compromise, their size against the megacorporations has caused their corporate policies to merely check for mental stability.  
Analysts in the coreworlds who know their name call them crazy, they call themselves and their Alliance successful.

Reasoning for being within the Alliance:  
Gaining some amount of traction among the drifters to which their progressive corporate interests benefited, they realized there was an immense power vaccum that the lack of megacorpo and nation-pact interests let them exploit. After several years of painful negotiations with fellow small organizations, and one accidental civil war, the Alliance looked upon the world with new eyes. A slice of the galaxy, with much time before hungrier factions gnaw into them.

Should arguments ensue that Management is unable to reconcile, the Captain has veto power against or for any topics and succestions.
